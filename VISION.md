# Vision: Swalpa Fast, the delivery rider's Bengaluru

**The name.** The game is "Swalpa Fast" (ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಫಾಸ್ಟ್, "a little fast": what every customer asks of
every rider). "Deliver It" is the delivery company inside the game, on the jacket, the box, the
dark store and the rider's app, and nowhere else.

## In one line
Be a delivery rider in a real, living Bengaluru. Beat the clock, keep customers happy,
survive the traffic, and slowly build a better life. Free in your browser.

**Short pitch:** GTA Bengaluru, but you're the delivery guy.

## What the game is
- **Lead: B, the rider's life.** A grounded, warm life sim about gig work: shifts,
  ratings, tips, EMIs, rentals, family, saving for something that matters.
- **A lot of A, funny and chaotic moments.** Ridiculous customers, rain, jams, cows,
  cakes on bumpy roads, guards asking for the OTP. Every order should risk a story.
- **C later, freedom with friends.** Exploring, any vehicle in Explore mode, and
  multiplayer rooms come after the core loop is fun.

## What a player should feel after 10 minutes
"That was funny, a bit stressful, and now I get what delivery riders go through.
One more order."

## The core loop
Go online → accept an order → ride through real streets → handle something funny or
hard → hand it over → get rated and paid → spend on vehicles and life → repeat.
Every feature must make this loop more fun, more varied or more meaningful.

## Three pillars
1. **Real Bengaluru.** Real HSR and Agara from map data, landmarks, Kannada and
   English signs, Kanglish voices, BMTC buses, autos, rain at four o'clock.
2. **The rider's life.** Money, ratings, fuel, rentals, upgrades, family messages,
   a savings goal, the dispatcher Ravi, other riders. Setbacks, never dead ends.
3. **Funny and true.** Humour from real situations, never mean. Customers, traffic
   and the city are the comedy; the rider is the hero.

## Not this game
- Weapons, violence, crime, police chases or wanted levels.
- A huge supercar garage. Vehicles exist because a rider would use them.
- Copying the other Bengaluru open-world games' signature features.
- Real brand names, copyrighted music, disrespect towards temples or communities.

## How to decide on a new feature
Ask, in order:
1. Does it make deliveries more fun, varied or meaningful?
2. Does it make Bengaluru feel more real?
3. Does it create a funny or memorable moment players would share?
4. Does it keep the game fast in a browser (High ≥ 80 FPS, load ≤ 8 s)?
If the answer to 1–3 is no, it waits for later.

## Priorities
- **Now:** a polished core loop and a great first five minutes (open on an order,
  ride, a funny doorstep scene, a tip, then "you're free to explore"). Fix mouse-look,
  getting stuck, and the safety net so players never get stuck broke.
- **Next:** sound and voices, daily challenge and share card,
  touch controls for phones, then a public launch.
- **Later:** multiplayer rooms, Explore mode with any vehicle, more neighbourhoods,
  parody delivery brands, the van and bulk orders.

## Players we're making it for
Bengaluru people who order online every day, young players who want to explore
their own city, and anyone curious about the people who bring their orders.
