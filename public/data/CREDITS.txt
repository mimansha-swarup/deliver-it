Map data used in Deliver It
===========================

hsr-compact.geojson, hsr-barriers.geojson
  Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

hsr-extra-buildings.geojson
  Buildings that OpenStreetMap doesn't have yet, from Overture Maps Foundation (buildings theme,
  release 2026-10-21.0), traced from satellite images by:
  - Google Open Buildings, licensed under CC BY 4.0. https://sites.research.google/open-buildings/
  - Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints, licensed under ODbL.
    https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints
  Overture Maps: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/
  Made with scripts/prepare_extra_buildings.py.

hsr-extra-worship.geojson
  Temples, mosques and churches OpenStreetMap doesn't have or hasn't named, from Overture Maps
  Foundation (places theme, release 2026-10-21.0), licensed under CDLA Permissive 2.0, with
  sources including Meta and Microsoft. https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/
  Made with scripts/prepare_extra_places.py.
